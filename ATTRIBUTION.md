NASA content is generally not subject to copyright in the United States ([NASA Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)). This project credits NASA and the missions below. **Use of NASA data does not imply endorsement by NASA.** The NASA insignia and logotype are not used in the game.

The machine-readable list (exact URLs, query parameters and retrieval dates) is in `public/assets/provenance.json`, and it is shown in-game under **Credits & NASA Data**.

## NASA Solar System Treks (trek.nasa.gov), NASA/JPL-Caltech
- **LRO LROC NAC DEM, Apollo 15** (`LRO_NAC_DEM_26N004E_150cmp`): LRO / Arizona State University (LROC), via Moon Trek ImageServer
- **LRO LOLA global DEM 256 ppd** (`LRO_LOLA_DEM_Global_256ppd_v06`): NASA GSFC LOLA team
- **LRO LROC NAC Image Mosaic, Apollo 15, 50 cm/px** (`LRO_NAC_Mosaic_26N004E_50cmp`): NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University
- **LRO LROC WAC Global Mosaic 303 ppd**: NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University
- **Kaguya Multiband Imager mineral maps** (FeO, plagioclase, clinopyroxene, olivine, optical maturity): JAXA/SELENE, via Moon Trek
- **Lunar Prospector GRS thorium**: NASA Lunar Prospector, via Moon Trek
- **Apollo 15 and Apollo 11 waypoints, Apollo 15 traverse paths, and bookmark descriptions**: Moon Trek
- **Gale Crater DEM 1 m** (`Gale_DEM_SMG_1m`): NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona (HiRISE) and MSSS (CTX), via Mars Trek
- **MOLA + HRSC blended DEM 200 m**: NASA MGS MOLA / ESA-DLR-FU Berlin Mars Express HRSC, via Mars Trek
- **HiRISE mosaic of the Curiosity landing area**: NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona, via Mars Trek
- **Viking MDIM 2.1 colour mosaic**: NASA/USGS, via Mars Trek
- **Curiosity waypoints, Curiosity traverse, and feature descriptions**: Mars Trek

## NASA 3D Resources (science.nasa.gov/3d-resources)
Apollo Lunar Module · Saturn V · Ingenuity Mars Helicopter · Mars 2020 Perseverance Rover · Viking Lander · Z2 Spacesuit (the last three as base exhibits). The Astronaut model was downloaded but is not shown: animated characters use procedural models. All were optimized (simplified, textures resized) for low-end PCs.

## api.nasa.gov
- InSight: Mars Weather Service API (NASA/JPL-Caltech; TWINS: CAB (CSIC-INTA)): archived sols
- APOD: Astronomy Picture of the Day (image credits as given by APOD)

## NASA Planetary Data System (pds.nasa.gov)
PDS4 context records retrieved through the PDS Search API: LRO LOLA, LRO LROC, the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter mission, Mars Science Laboratory, MRO HiRISE and the Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter. The Trek DEMs and mosaics above are derived from PDS-archived products.

## data.nasa.gov
Apollo 15 Lunar Sample Information Catalog record, which links to NASA JSC Curation. Sample facts come from the **Lunar Sample Compendium** by C. Meyer, NASA JSC (curator.jsc.nasa.gov/lunar/lsc): samples 15415, 15555 and 15016.

## NASA Image and Video Library (images.nasa.gov)
"The Blue Marble", AS17-148-22727, Apollo 17 crew, 7 December 1972.

## Physical constants
NASA Planetary Fact Sheet (nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet): surface gravity, radii, atmosphere.

## Original work in this project
Code, procedural 3D models (astronaut, LRV-2, ALSEP, debris, landers, station modules, launch tower), the UI and all synthesized audio are original. Characters, bases and the Pathfinder-H programme are fictional.
